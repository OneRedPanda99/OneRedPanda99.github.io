About

My name is Parker

I'm a senior at Cardinal Newman planning to study mechatronics engineering. I've been teaching myself technical skills since I was 10, when I set up my first Minecraft server. Since then I've moved on to designing parts in Fusion 360, building websites, and working to start an FTC robotics team at my school. Outside of tech, I'm the stage manager for our school theater and the department's photographer. I'm a perfectionist: when something doesn't work the way I picture it, I keep at it until it does.
